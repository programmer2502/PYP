Frequently Asked Questions

🛡️ How does Escrow Protection work?

When you book a photographer, your payment is held in a secure escrow vault. The funds are only transferred to the creator once you review and approve the final deliverables.

📅 What is the cancellation policy?

Full refund if cancelled up to 48 hours prior to the scheduled shoot date. Rescheduling is 100% free with creator mutual consent.

☁️ How do I receive my photos?

Photographers upload high-resolution color-graded files directly to your cloud gallery, available in single-tap high-res view and full master ZIP download.
